# notes
Back in 2019 stephen snigrev, a Quantum phycists who had built the great uBitcoin library for Spector wallet.

I was there retro-fitting arcade machines to accept bitcoin payments it what later became the bitcoinSwitch project.

We put some tables together and started what became a tradition of hackspaces at the bitcoin conference.

We relealised we could use uBitcoin and esp32s excellenet TRNG to make very affordable hardware wallets for a few dollars. Great for unbanked regions, great as they would have no supply chain attack, and great as an education tool. We also committed ourselves to nurturing a makerscene encouraging bitcoiners to make their own hardware, which was a great success.

Slide 2
THE BOWSER WALLET WAS BORN
<pic bowser-born> <pic vlad> <pic blackcoffee> <pic yvette>
"
# notes
A year later i made bowser wallet, beta, and as a fun educational tool. I met Vlad Stan through Bowser wallet who helped make a client  for it, Vlad Stan is a bitcoinjs maintainer who now works on LNbits. When Vlad Stan was full time on LNbits, and being a genius he rebuilt Bowser into a fantastic hardware wallet, the name was bad tho, we just called it "hardware wallet" intending it to be generic enough people could fork and use as a base. Via 100s of workshops folkls from Lnbits community, blackcoffee, Yvette taught many people about diy hardware wallets.

More recently the coldcard debacle has made me relise I need to give the project more exposure, so i have chnaged the name back to Bowser, and I am making this tutorial.
Snigrev stepped back from the bitcoin space, which is a real shame and went back to building quantum computers. We stand on the shoulders of giants.

The Bowser wallet uses intentionally simple and generic hardware you can source anywhere. You can buy it from our shop, but please dont do that, buy from aliexpress or amazon.

The security model of a bowser is you keys being on a very simple FLOSS stack, it has no secure element, so keep it locked away in a safe with the the gold bars. Secure elements have always been lower on the list for my own security model as I keep my hardware wallets extremely secure.
"

Webserial mode
* Connects via usb to the computer
* Fine for smaller amounts up to $10,000s
* TRNG: ESP32 TRNG is one of worlds most proved and tested
* TRNG: Transparent and easy to find code
* TRNG: Lots of entropy inputs

SD Card mode
* Commands are passed via sd card txt files
* air-gapped and simple for minimal attack vector
* Dice: infinitly easy to audit
* Dice: Only calls a few functions (SHA256 etc)
